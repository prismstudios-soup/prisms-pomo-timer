Privacy Policy

Prism's Pomo Timer · Effective 3 October 2026

Prism's Pomo Timer is a focus timer. It has no user accounts and no Prism's Pomo Timer server. We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

  • Completed focus sessions, daily goals, planned sessions, and timer settings.
  • Background, sound, and animation preferences.
  • Music files you choose to add. These remain in the app's private storage on your device and are not uploaded by Prism's Pomo Timer.
  • Progress toward temporary ad-free access.

You can delete this local data by clearing Prism's Pomo Timer storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Notifications

Timer-completion and planned-session reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can disable them in the app or Android settings.

Block apps

If you enable Block apps, the app uses Android Usage Access to detect when a selected app is opened during an active focus session so that it can show a focus reminder. This information is used on your device and can be disabled through Android settings.

Advertising

The free version of Prism's Pomo Timer uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. This may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit.

You can manage advertising preferences, reset your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation through Android's Google advertising settings. Google’s privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Prism's Pomo Timer is not directed to children under 13.
